Pengana International Equities Limited (PIA)

Pengana International Equities Limited (ASX: PIA) is a small-cap, Australian-listed investment company that provides diversified exposure to international equities through its investment portfolio managed by Pengana Capital. It operates primarily through its investment portfolio, focusing on opportunities globally, excluding Australia. Key services include professional investment management and regular income distributions to shareholders.

Is PIA a good investment?

What drives PIA's share price?

PIA's share price is primarily driven by changes in its NAV, global equity market trends, and to a lesser extent, the Australian dollar's strength against other currencies.
